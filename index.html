Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it really work 100% without internet connection? expand_more
Yes! Alyn's POS uses a resilient offline-first local SQLite architecture. All sales transactions, customers, and stock logs are stored locally on your Android tablet or phone. Whenever your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, it safely syncs everything to the cloud automatically.
Can I connect a 58mm or 80mm Bluetooth thermal receipt printer? expand_more
Yes. The app is compatible with standard ESC/POS Bluetooth and USB thermal printers commonly sold in the Philippines (e.g. POS-58, Xprinter, Milestone).
How do I install the APK on my Android device? expand_more
Tap any "Download APK" button on this website from your Android device. Once downloaded, open the file from your Notifications or Downloads folder. If prompted, enable "Install unknown apps" in your Android settings, and tap Install.
Can multiple cashiers log in with different PIN codes? expand_more
Yes. You can create multiple cashier profiles with custom 4-digit PINs. Cashier transactions are attributed to each person so end-of-day cash drawer balancing is clear and tamper-proof.
